**Internuclear Ophthalmoplegia (INO) is caused by a lesion in the Medial Longitudinal Fasciculus (MLF).**
**Key Sign:** The ipsilateral eye cannot adduct, while the contralateral eye abducts with nystagmus. Convergence is preserved.
**Classic Association:** Multiple Sclerosis (MS).
