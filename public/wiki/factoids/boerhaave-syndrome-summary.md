**Boerhaave syndrome is a transmural esophageal rupture following forceful vomiting, typically causing left-sided pleural effusion and sepsis.**
**Key Diagnosis:** Water-soluble contrast esophagography (shows leak).
**Key Sign:** Hamman sign (crunching sound) and pneumomediastinum.
