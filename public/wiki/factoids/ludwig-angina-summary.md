**Ludwig Angina is a rapidly progressive cellulitis of the submandibular/sublingual spaces, usually stemming from infected mandibular molars.**
**Classic Sign:** Tongue displaced superiorly/posteriorly with submandibular induration and potential crepitus.
**Critical Action:** Prompt airway assessment and IV antibiotics to prevent obstruction.
