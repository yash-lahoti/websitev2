**TCA overdose presents with anticholinergic symptoms, seizures, and QRS prolongation (>100ms).**
**Key Treatment:** IV sodium bicarbonate is the first-line therapy for QRS widening and ventricular arrhythmias to overcome sodium channel blockade.
