**Zenker diverticulum is a pulsion pseudodiverticulum caused by cricopharyngeus dysfunction.**
**Key Sign:** Regurgitation of undigested food, halitosis, and a posterior pouch on barium esophagogram.
**Treatment:** Cricopharyngeal myotomy.
