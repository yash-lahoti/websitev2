# Factoid: Myotonic Dystrophy Clinical Triad

**One-liner:** Myotonic dystrophy classically presents with a combination of distal muscle weakness, grip myotonia, and multi-systemic involvement (e.g., cataracts, testicular atrophy).

**Expand:** 
Unlike other muscular dystrophies that focus on proximal weakness (like Duchenne), DM targets the face and distal limbs. The hallmark "myotonia" is a failure of the muscle to relax immediately after a voluntary contraction or percussion. The associated systemic features (cataracts, endocrine failure, cardiac conduction issues) are critical for differentiating it from pure myopathies.
