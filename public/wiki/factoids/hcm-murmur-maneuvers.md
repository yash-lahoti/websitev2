**The murmur of Hypertrophic Cardiomyopathy (HCM) increases in intensity during the Valsalva strain phase and when standing, as both decrease LV volume and worsen LVOT obstruction.**
**Opposite of Aortic Stenosis**, where preload increase (squatting) increases murmur intensity.
