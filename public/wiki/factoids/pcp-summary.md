**PCP is an opportunistic infection in patients with severe cell-mediated immunity deficits (AIDS CD4 <200 or chronic steroids).**
**Key Findings:** Fever, dry cough, $\uparrow$ LDH, and bilateral diffuse interstitial infiltrates on CXR.
**Treatment:** Trimethoprim-sulfamethoxazole (TMP-SMX).
