# Factoid: ILD and Gas Exchange

**One-liner:** Interstitial Lung Disease causes an increased A-a gradient and hypoxemia, but hypercapnia is typically a late finding.

**Expand:** 
The thickened alveolar-capillary membrane (due to collagen deposition) impairs the diffusion of oxygen. However, $\text{CO}_2$ is significantly more soluble and diffuses much more rapidly than $\text{O}_2$, allowing $\text{CO}_2$ to be exhaled effectively even when $\text{O}_2$ uptake is compromised. Additionally, the absence of air trapping (unlike in emphysema) facilitates $\text{CO}_2$ clearance.
