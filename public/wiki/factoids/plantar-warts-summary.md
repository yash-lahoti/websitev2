**Plantar warts (HPV) present as verrucous, hyperkeratotic papules on the soles of the feet.**
**Key Diagnostic:** Shaving the lesion reveals **pinpoint bleeding** from thrombosed capillaries.
**Treatment:** Cryotherapy or salicylic acid.
