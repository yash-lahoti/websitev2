**Infertility is defined as $\ge 12$ months of unprotected intercourse (women < 35). The first step is often semen analysis due to the 40% prevalence of male factor infertility.**
**Key Tests:** Semen analysis (male), midluteal progesterone (ovulation), HSG (tubal patency).
