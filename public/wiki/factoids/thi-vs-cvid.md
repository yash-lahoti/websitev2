# Factoid: THI vs CVID

**One-liner:** The key differentiator between Transient Hypogammaglobulinemia of Infancy (THI) and Common Variable Immunodeficiency (CVID) is the response to vaccines.

**Expand:** 
Both conditions present with low IgG levels. However, infants with THI retain the ability to produce specific antibodies in response to vaccinations (e.g., tetanus, diphtheria), whereas patients with CVID have a defective antibody response. THI is self-limiting, while CVID is a lifelong deficiency.
