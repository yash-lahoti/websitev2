**Ankylosing spondylitis can cause Aortic Regurgitation (AR) via chronic inflammation of the aortic root and valve cusps.**
**Key Sign:** In an AS patient, exertional dyspnea and lateral displacement of the PMI suggest AR-induced heart failure.
