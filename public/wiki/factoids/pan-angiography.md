# PAN Angiography

**One-liner:** Polyarteritis nodosa (PAN) angiography typically shows "beading" consisting of microaneurysms, irregular luminal narrowing, and distal occlusions of medium-sized arteries.

**Expand:** This pattern occurs because the transmural inflammation damages the internal and external elastic laminae of the arterial wall.
