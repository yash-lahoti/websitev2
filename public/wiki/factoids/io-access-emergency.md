**Intraosseous (IO) access is the preferred next step for emergency fluid resuscitation when peripheral IV access fails and the patient is in shock.**
**Preferred Site:** Proximal tibia.
**Key Advantage:** Faster and safer to place than a central venous catheter in an emergency.
