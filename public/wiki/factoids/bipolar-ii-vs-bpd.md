**Bipolar II is defined by a history of $\ge 1$ major depressive episode and $\ge 1$ hypomanic episode ($\ge 4$ days), without any history of full mania.**
**Contrast with BPD:** Bipolar mood shifts last weeks/months; BPD mood shifts last hours/days.
