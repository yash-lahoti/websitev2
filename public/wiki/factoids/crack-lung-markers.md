# Factoid: "Crack Lung" Markers

**One-liner:** Fingertip burns in a patient with acute respiratory distress and sympathomimetic signs are a highly specific indicator of inhaled cocaine-induced lung toxicity.

**Expand:** 
The burns result from handling hot pipes. Because cocaine blunts nociception (pain perception), users often don't realize they are burning their fingers. This clinical clue, combined with diffuse alveolar opacities and normal cardiac biomarkers, strongly points to cocaine-induced DAH over cardiogenic pulmonary edema.
