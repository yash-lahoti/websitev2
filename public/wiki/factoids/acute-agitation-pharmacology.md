**For acutely agitated patients where verbal de-escalation fails, the first-line pharmacological choice is a rapid-acting benzodiazepine (e.g., lorazepam) or an antipsychotic.**
**Avoid:** Mood stabilizers (Lithium/Valproate) as they are not indicated for acute crisis stabilization.
