**Complex Regional Pain Syndrome (CRPS) is characterized by severe burning pain, allodynia, and edema following trauma. X-rays often show patchy demineralization.**
**Key Differentiator:** Pain is regional (not dermatomal) and disproportionate to the inciting event.
