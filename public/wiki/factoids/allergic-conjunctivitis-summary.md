**Allergic conjunctivitis presents with bilateral redness, watery discharge, and intense pruritus. Treatment includes topical antihistamines and cool compresses.**
**Key Differentiator:** Pruritus is the hallmark of allergic vs viral/bacterial conjunctivitis.
