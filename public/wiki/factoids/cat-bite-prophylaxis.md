# Factoid: Cat Bite Prophylaxis

**One-liner:** All cat bites warrant antibiotic prophylaxis with amoxicillin-clavulanate due to the high risk of *Pasteurella multocida* infection from deep puncture wounds.

**Expand:** 
Cats have long, slender teeth that create narrow, deep tracts, effectively injecting oral bacteria into deep tissues, tendon sheaths, and joints. *Pasteurella multocida* is the predominant pathogen. Amoxicillin provides the necessary gram-negative coverage, while clavulanate addresses beta-lactamase-producing anaerobes.
