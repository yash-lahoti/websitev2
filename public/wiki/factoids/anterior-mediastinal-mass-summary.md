**The "4 Ts" of anterior mediastinal masses are: Thymoma, Teratoma/GCT, "Terrible" Lymphoma, and Thyroid neoplasms.**
**Key Marker:** In malignant GCTs, elevated AFP indicates a nonseminomatous tumor (seminomas almost always have normal AFP).
