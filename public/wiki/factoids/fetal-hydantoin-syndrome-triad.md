**Fetal hydantoin syndrome (phenytoin, carbamazepine) presents with the classic triad of: microcephaly, wide anterior fontanelle, and distal phalange hypoplasia (often with cleft lip/palate).**
**Prevention:** High-dose folic acid (4mg) prior to conception.
