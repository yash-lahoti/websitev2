**Testicular torsion is a surgical emergency featuring abrupt severe scrotal pain, a high-riding testicle, and an absent cremasteric reflex.**
**Critical Marker:** Heterogeneous echotexture on ultrasound indicates necrosis (usually >12h ischemia).
