# Factoid: Prerenal AKI "Bland" Sediment

**One-liner:** A "bland" urine sediment (absence of casts, cells, or crystals) in the setting of azotemia is a hallmark of prerenal AKI.

**Expand:** 
Because prerenal AKI is a hemodynamic problem (decreased perfusion) rather than a structural one, the tubular epithelium remains intact. Therefore, there are no sloughed cells to form casts (unlike ATN) and no glomerular inflammation to cause RBC casts (unlike GN). If you see muddy brown casts, the "prerenal" state has already progressed to "intrinsic" (ATN).
