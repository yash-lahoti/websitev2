**Neonatal polycythemia (Hct > 65%) is caused by intrauterine hypoxia (e.g., maternal DM/HTN) or excessive transfusion. It presents with plethoric skin and can cause respiratory distress and hypoglycemia.**
**Key Marker:** Hematocrit > 65% with normal chest X-ray.
