**Todd paralysis is a transient focal weakness (e.g., hemiplegia) occurring postictally after a focal seizure. It is self-limited and usually resolves within 36 hours.**
**Key Marker:** Normal brain imaging (CT/MRI) and a clear history of a preceding seizure.
