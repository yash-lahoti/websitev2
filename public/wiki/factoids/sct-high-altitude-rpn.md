# Factoid: Sickle Cell Trait and High Altitude

**One-liner:** High altitude and dehydration can trigger sickling in patients with Sickle Cell Trait, leading specifically to renal papillary necrosis.

**Expand:** 
The renal medulla is naturally an environment of relative hypoxia and acidity. In a person with SCT, these conditions—exacerbated by high altitude (low $pO_2$) and dehydration—promote the polymerization of HbS. This leads to microvascular occlusion in the vasa recta, causing ischemia and necrosis of the renal papillae, which then slough into the collecting system, causing gross hematuria and flank pain.
