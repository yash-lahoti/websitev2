**Traumatic diaphragmatic rupture often follows blunt trauma and presents with bowel loops in the thorax and mediastinal shift on CXR.**
**Key Fact:** The left side is more commonly affected due to the liver's protection of the right side.
**Diagnosis:** CT chest/abdomen is the definitive diagnostic modality.
