**Lemierre syndrome is a primary oropharyngeal infection (usually *Fusobacterium necrophorum*) leading to internal jugular vein thrombosis and septic pulmonary emboli.**
**Classic Triad:** Sore throat $\rightarrow$ Sternocleidomastoid tenderness/swelling $\rightarrow$ Pulmonary nodules on CXR.
