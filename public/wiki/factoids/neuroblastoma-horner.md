**Neuroblastoma arises from neural crest cells and can present as Horner syndrome (ptosis, miosis, anhidrosis) if it involves the cervical paravertebral sympathetic chain.**
**Key Markers:** Elevated urine catecholamines (VMA/HVA) and "small round blue cells" on histology.
