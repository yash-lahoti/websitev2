# Factoid: Chronic DIC and Mucin Tumors

**One-liner:** Mucin-producing tumors (pancreatic, gastric, ovarian) can cause a chronic, low-grade DIC by periodically releasing tissue factor into the blood.

**Expand:** 
This "consumptive coagulopathy" typically presents as a paradox: the patient has a high risk of venous thromboembolism (e.g., DVT) but also exhibits periodic mucosal bleeding. Because the process is intermittent, the body can maintain normal levels of platelets and coagulation factors, making standard labs misleading. The hallmark is an elevated D-dimer.
