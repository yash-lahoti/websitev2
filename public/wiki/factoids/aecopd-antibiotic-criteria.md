# AECOPD Antibiotic Indication

**One-liner:** Antibiotics in AECOPD are indicated for patients requiring hospitalization or those with grossly purulent sputum.

**Expand:**
While many exacerbations are viral, severe cases or those with purulent sputum are more likely to have bacterial triggers (e.g., *H. influenzae*, *M. catarrhalis*). Empiric choices include respiratory fluoroquinolones or 3rd gen cephalosporins.
