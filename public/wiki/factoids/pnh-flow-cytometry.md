# PNH Flow Cytometry

**One-liner:** PNH is confirmed via flow cytometry showing the absence of CD55 and CD59 on RBCs.

**Expand:**
The defect is an acquired mutation in the GPI-anchor gene, preventing the attachment of complement-regulating proteins (CD55, CD59) to the cell membrane. This allows the complement membrane attack complex (MAC) to form freely, leading to intravascular hemolysis.
