# Factoid: Modafinil vs Stimulants in Narcolepsy

**One-liner:** Modafinil is preferred over traditional stimulants (amphetamines) as first-line therapy for narcolepsy due to a lower risk of abuse and fewer cardiovascular side effects.

**Expand:** 
While both Modafinil and traditional stimulants increase wakefulness, Modafinil has a more targeted effect on the arousal system and does not typically cause the severe tachycardia, hypertension, or appetite suppression associated with amphetamines. It is specifically indicated for the reduction of excessive daytime sleepiness.
