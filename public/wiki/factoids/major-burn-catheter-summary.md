**Major burn management (>20% TBSA) requires immediate urethral catheterization to monitor fluid resuscitation (Goal: $\ge 0.5$ mL/kg/hr).**
**Critical Timing:** Catheterize early, especially with genital burns, to avoid edema-induced obstruction.
