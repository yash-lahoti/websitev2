**Active phase protraction is <1 cm cervical dilation in 2 hours (from 6-10 cm).**
**Common Cause:** Cephalopelvic disproportion (fetal head too large for pelvis).
**Key Metric:** Adequate contractions are $\ge$ 200 Montevideo units; if present but progress is slow, suspect CPD.
