**Prompt:** A trauma patient presents with hypotension, tachycardia, diminished breath sounds, and contralateral tracheal deviation. How do you distinguish between a massive hemothorax and a tension pneumothorax on physical exam?

**Answer:** Use **percussion**. 
- **Massive Hemothorax** $\rightarrow$ **Dullness** to percussion.
- **Tension Pneumothorax** $\rightarrow$ **Hyperresonance** to percussion.
Additionally, check neck veins: flat in hemothorax (hypovolemic shock) vs. distended in tension pneumothorax (obstructive shock).
