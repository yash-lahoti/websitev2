**Prompt:** What is the most appropriate response to a patient who is hesitant to attempt smoking cessation again because they do not want to relapse and "be seen as a failure"?

**Answer:** Reassure the patient that smoking cessation failure is common, that it may take several attempts to achieve long-term success, and that over half of smokers eventually quit successfully.
