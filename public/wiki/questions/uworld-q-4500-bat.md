**Prompt:** A patient with blunt abdominal trauma is hemodynamically unstable despite initial fluid resuscitation. FAST is positive for free intraperitoneal fluid. There is no evidence of peritonitis. What is the best next step?

**Answer:** **Emergent laparotomy**. 
In an unstable patient, a positive FAST scan is sufficient to indicate immediate surgical intervention to control intraabdominal hemorrhage.
