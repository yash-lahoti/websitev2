**Prompt:** A patient has dullness to percussion in the right lower lung field. How can you distinguish between a pleural effusion and lobar pneumonia (consolidation) on physical exam?

**Answer:** Check **tactile fremitus** and **breath sounds**.
- **Pleural Effusion:** $\downarrow$ breath sounds and $\downarrow$ tactile fremitus (fluid insulates sound/vibration).
- **Consolidation:** $\uparrow$ breath sounds and $\uparrow$ tactile fremitus (solid tissue transmits sound/vibration more efficiently than air).
