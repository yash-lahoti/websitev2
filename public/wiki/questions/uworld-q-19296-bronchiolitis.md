**Prompt:** An 18-month-old boy presents with fever, cough, and nasal congestion that has progressed to wheezing and tachypnea. Chest X-ray shows peribronchial cuffing. He is alert, playful, and has an oxygen saturation of 96%. What is the best next step in management?

**Answer:** **Discharge with close follow-up**.
Because the patient is stable (well-hydrated, $\text{SpO}_2 > 92\%$, and mild work of breathing), supportive care (suctioning, hydration) provided as an outpatient is sufficient.
