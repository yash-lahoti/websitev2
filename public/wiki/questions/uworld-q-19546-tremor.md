**Prompt:** A patient presents with a hand tremor that is present at rest and during movement, but ceases when the clinician performs other parts of the neurological exam. The tremor frequency shifts when the patient is asked to tap their foot. What is the most likely diagnosis?

**Answer:** **Functional tremor**. 
Key identifiers:
1. **Distractibility:** Ceases during other exam tasks.
2. **Inconsistency:** Shifting frequency (entrainment) and atypical presentation (both rest and action).
