**Prompt:** A patient presents with encephalopathy, high-anion gap metabolic acidosis, acute kidney injury, and calcium oxalate crystals in the urine. What is the most likely diagnosis and the preferred antidote?

**Answer:** Ethylene glycol toxicity. The preferred antidote is **fomepizole**, which inhibits alcohol dehydrogenase.
