**Prompt:** An incidental 1.5-cm coin-shaped peripheral lung nodule is found on a chest X-ray in a patient with no history of smoking or malignancy. What is the first step in management?

**Answer:** Obtain and compare with previous radiographic images. Stability over 2-3 years effectively rules out malignancy.
