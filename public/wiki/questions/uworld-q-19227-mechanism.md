**Prompt:** Why does a patient with sepsis and renal insufficiency develop hypoglycemia?

**Answer:** Due to increased glucose utilization by tissues (promoted by inflammatory cytokines) and suppressed hepatic and renal gluconeogenesis, combined with reduced renal clearance of endogenous insulin.
