**Prompt:** How do you calculate Relative Risk Reduction (RRR) given the event rates of a control group and a treatment group?

**Answer:** $\text{RRR} = \frac{\text{Control Rate} - \text{Treatment Rate}}{\text{Control Rate}}$. Alternatively, if you have the Relative Risk (RR), $\text{RRR} = 1 - \text{RR}$.
