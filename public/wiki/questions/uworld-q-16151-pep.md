**Prompt:** A patient develops severe epigastric pain radiating to the back and nausea 24 hours after an ERCP for sphincter of Oddi dysfunction. What is the most appropriate next diagnostic step?

**Answer:** **Serum lipase** (and amylase). 
Because the diagnosis of acute pancreatitis requires $\ge 2$ of the 3 criteria (pain, enzymes, imaging), and enzymes rise faster than CT changes occur, lipase is the most appropriate initial test.
