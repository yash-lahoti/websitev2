**Prompt:** A patient's family is reluctant to withdraw life support for a terminally ill patient despite medical futility, fearing guilt over "giving up." What is the most appropriate next step to align goals of care?

**Answer:** Reframe the discussion around **No Escalation of Treatment (NEOT)** orders. This sets limits on future interventions (e.g., no new ventilators or dialysis) without requiring the immediate withdrawal of current support, reducing guilt and preventing futile escalation.
