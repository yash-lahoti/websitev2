**Prompt:** What is the underlying pathophysiologic mechanism of acute graft-versus-host disease (GVHD) after a bone marrow transplant?

**Answer:** Donor T-lymphocytes recognize and react against the host's major and minor HLA-antigens, leading to a cell-mediated immune response against host tissues.
