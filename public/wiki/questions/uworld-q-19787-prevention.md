**Prompt:** A patient suffers an adverse drug event due to a vague prescription and a lack of medication counseling. Which intervention is most likely to prevent this type of error?

**Answer:** Providing plain-language medication teaching and counseling to improve patient health literacy and ensure clear understanding of dosing limits and intervals.
