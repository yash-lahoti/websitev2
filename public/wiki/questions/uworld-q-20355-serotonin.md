**Prompt:** A patient on fluoxetine presents with agitation, hyperthermia, tachycardia, hyperreflexia, and ankle clonus after starting a new over-the-counter cough medication (dextromethorphan). What is the most appropriate initial pharmacologic therapy?

**Answer:** **Benzodiazepines** (e.g., lorazepam). 
They are used to treat agitation, decrease muscle contractions (which reduces hyperthermia), and stabilize blood pressure and heart rate. If the patient is unresponsive to BZDs and supportive care, cyproheptadine is the next step.
