**Prompt:** When is randomization ethically permissible in a randomized controlled trial (RCT)?

**Answer:** Randomization is permissible only when there is **clinical equipoise**, meaning there is genuine uncertainty regarding the comparative effectiveness of the experimental intervention versus the control intervention.
