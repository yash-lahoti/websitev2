**Prompt:** A patient presents with extensive, widespread scaly annular plaques on the chest and abdomen. KOH prep is positive for hyphae. In addition to antifungal treatment, what else should be performed?

**Answer:** Screen for underlying immunocompromising conditions, specifically an HIV screening test, as widespread tinea corporis is associated with compromised immunity.
