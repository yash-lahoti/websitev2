**Prompt:** A 4-year-old girl has recurrent cystitis and a history of functional constipation. What is the mechanism by which her constipation contributes to the urinary tract infections?

**Answer:** **Urinary stasis**. 
Impacted stool in a dilated rectum compresses the bladder and urethra, preventing complete voiding. This leads to urinary retention/stasis, which allows ascending bacteria to colonize the bladder.
