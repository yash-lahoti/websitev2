**Prompt:** Which nonpharmacologic intervention is likely to have the greatest impact on reducing systolic blood pressure in a patient with primary hypertension?

**Answer:** The **DASH (Dietary Approaches to Stop Hypertension) diet**, which can reduce systolic blood pressure by approximately 11 mm Hg.
