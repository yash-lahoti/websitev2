**Prompt:** What is the most likely pathophysiologic mechanism responsible for the triad of gait instability, cognitive decline, and urinary urgency in an elderly patient?

**Answer:** Decreased absorption of cerebrospinal fluid (CSF) by the arachnoid granulations, leading to ventricular enlargement (Normal Pressure Hydrocephalus).
