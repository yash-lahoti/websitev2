**Prompt:** A patient with alcohol use disorder acknowledges the need to stop but is ambivalent about inpatient treatment, stating they might consider outpatient counseling. What is the most appropriate response?

**Answer:** Use a motivational interviewing approach: explore the patient's perspective on outpatient treatment (e.g., "Tell me some ways that outpatient alcohol treatment might help you"), discuss the pros and cons of the option, and assess their conviction and confidence.
