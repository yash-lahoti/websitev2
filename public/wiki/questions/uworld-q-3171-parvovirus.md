**Prompt:** A patient presents with symmetric small-joint arthralgias and mild swelling in the hands. She has a history of working in a daycare. Morning stiffness lasts 10 minutes and her ESR is normal. What is the most likely diagnosis?

**Answer:** **Viral arthritis (likely Parvovirus B19)**.
The combination of acute onset, brief morning stiffness, normal ESR, and exposure to children strongly suggests a self-limited viral arthritis rather than a chronic inflammatory process like Rheumatoid Arthritis.
