**Prompt:** A 36-year-old man presents with minimal bright red blood on the toilet paper. He has no systemic symptoms, no change in bowel habits, and a normal digital rectal exam. What is the most appropriate next step in management?

**Answer:** **Anoscopy**.
In young patients with minimal BRBPR and no "red flags" (no blood mixed with stool, no weight loss, age < 45), initial evaluation should start with a less invasive method to visualize the anal canal before proceeding to colonoscopy.
