**Prompt:** In a prospective cohort study, 40% of the exposed group and 36% of the unexposed group are lost to follow-up. Why does this create a risk of bias, and what is this specific bias called?

**Answer:** This is **attrition bias** (a subtype of **selection bias**). It occurs because the participants who dropped out may differ systematically in their risk of the outcome compared to those who remained, potentially leading to an overestimation or underestimation of the association between exposure and disease.
