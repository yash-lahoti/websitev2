**Prompt:** A patient is at 41 weeks gestation. If delivery is delayed to 42 weeks, what is the primary risk associated with progressive placental dysfunction?

**Answer:** **Oligohydramnios**.
As placental function declines, blood is shunted to the fetal brain and away from the kidneys, reducing urine production and amniotic fluid volume. This serves as a marker for uteroplacental insufficiency and an indication for delivery.
