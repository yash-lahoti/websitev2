**Prompt:** A woman with known antiphospholipid antibody syndrome (APS) requires a highly effective form of contraception. Which method is most appropriate, and which is absolutely contraindicated?

**Answer:** A **copper-containing IUD** (or progestin-only method) is most appropriate. **Estrogen-containing contraceptives** (e.g., combination pills, rings, patches) are absolutely contraindicated due to the high risk of thromboembolism.
