# Diffuse Alveolar Hemorrhage (DAH)

## Summary
A clinical syndrome characterized by widespread bleeding into the alveoli, leading to rapid-onset hypoxemia and diffuse pulmonary opacities.

## Etiologies
- **Pulmonary Capillaritis:** ANCA vasculitis, SLE, antiphospholipid antibodies.
- **Bland Hemorrhage:** Mitral stenosis, anticoagulation.
- **Alveolar Damage:** Viral pneumonitis, ARDS, drug-induced (e.g., cocaine, amiodarone).

## Presentation / Diagnosis
- **Symptoms:** Dyspnea, hypoxemia. Hemoptysis may be present but is absent in $\sim 50\%$ of patients.
- **Imaging:** Diffuse ground-glass opacities on CXR or CT.
- **Gold Standard:** Bronchoscopy with serial lavage (sequential blood in vials).

## Management
- Treat underlying cause (e.g., immunosuppressants for vasculitis).
- Supportive care (Oxygen/Ventilation).
- **Avoid anticoagulation.**
