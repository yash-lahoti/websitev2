# Bronchiolitis

## Overview
Bronchiolitis is a common lower respiratory tract infection in children **< 2 years old**, most frequently caused by **Respiratory Syncytial Virus (RSV)**. It is characterized by inflammation and obstruction of the small distal airways.

## Clinical Presentation
- **Prodrome:** Starts with upper respiratory symptoms (cough, nasal congestion, rhinorrhea).
- **Progression:** Progresses to lower respiratory symptoms, typically peaking between **days 3 and 5**.
- **Physical Exam:**
    - Tachypnea, retractions, and increased work of breathing.
    - Scattered wheezing and crackles on auscultation.
    - May present with hypoxia and fever.

## Diagnostic Findings
- **Diagnosis:** Primarily clinical.
- **Chest X-ray (CXR):** Often performed to exclude pneumonia or in cases of severe/atypical progression. Findings include:
    - **Hyperinflation** (due to air trapping).
    - **Increased interstitial markings**.
    - **Peribronchial cuffing** (haziness of bronchial walls).
    - Focal consolidation is typically **absent**.

## Management
The mainstay of treatment is **supportive care**.

### Stable Patients (Outpatient)
Patients who are well-hydrated, have adequate oxygenation ($\text{SpO}_2 > 92\%$), and exhibit only mild/intermittent respiratory distress can be managed as outpatients with:
- **Nasal suctioning**.
- **Maintaining hydration**.
- **Close follow-up**.

### Severe Patients (Inpatient)
Patients with severe hypoxia, apnea, or persistent respiratory distress require hospitalization for:
- Supplemental oxygen.
- IV fluids for hydration.
- Respiratory support (e.g., CPAP) in severe cases.

### Ineffective/Limited Therapies
- **Antibiotics:** Not indicated unless a secondary bacterial pneumonia is suspected.
- **Glucocorticoids/Bronchodilators:** Generally not recommended for first-time episodes. Albuterol may be considered in patients with a history of recurrent viral-triggered wheezing.

## Prevention
- **Nirsevimab:** Monoclonal antibody administered to all infants $< 8$ months to prevent severe RSV.
