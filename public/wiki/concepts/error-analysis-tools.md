# Error Analysis Tools

## Root Cause Analysis (RCA)
A comprehensive, **retrospective** tool used to identify all underlying causes of a specific error or series of errors.
- **Objective:** Find all underlying reasons (both active and latent factors).
- **Active Factors:** Human errors, incorrect actions of personnel (readily apparent).
- **Latent Factors:** System-level deficiencies (e.g., confusing controls, poor protocols).
- **Methods:** 
	- **5 Whys:** Iteratively asking "why" until the root cause is found.
	- **Fishbone Diagram:** Visual depiction of relationships among factors (personnel, device, environment).
	- **Process Mapping:** Visual outline of steps to identify where errors occurred.

## Failure Mode and Effects Analysis (FMEA)
A **prospective** review conducted *before* errors occur.
- **Objective:** Identify system vulnerabilities and the likelihood of potential errors within a process.
- **Use Case:** Guiding the development of new organizational protocols or processes.

## Common Cause Analysis
A **retrospective** review that aggregates data from multiple error events.
- **Objective:** Identify the most common trends and themes across multiple errors.
- **Use Case:** Prioritizing safety improvements at the organizational or environmental level.

## Morbidity & Mortality (M&M) Conference
A **retrospective**, confidential, interdisciplinary group discussion.
- **Objective:** Identify system-level improvements to address a specific error.
