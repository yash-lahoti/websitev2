# Postpartum GDM Screening

## Summary
Patients diagnosed with Gestational Diabetes Mellitus (GDM) have a proven predisposition toward insulin resistance and an increased risk of developing Type 2 Diabetes Mellitus (T2DM).

## Screening Protocol
- **Timing:** 6–12 weeks postpartum.
- **Test:** **2-hour (75-g) oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT)**.
- **Follow-up:** If the initial postpartum screening is negative, repeat screening should occur at **3-year intervals**.

## Clinical Pearls
- **Lochia:** Scant brown vaginal bleeding up to 8 weeks postpartum is typically normal lochia and does not require endometrial biopsy unless other red flags are present.
- **Screeing Rationale:** Some GDM cases are actually undiagnosed pre-existing T2DM; others develop T2DM due to the metabolic stress of pregnancy.

## Common Traps
- Choosing a 1-hour GCT instead of a 2-hour OGTT for postpartum follow-up.
- Over-diagnosing abnormal bleeding as a pathology when it is consistent with normal lochia.
