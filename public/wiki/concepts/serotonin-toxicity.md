# Serotonin Toxicity

## Overview
Serotonin toxicity is a potentially life-threatening condition caused by excessive serotonergic activity in the central and peripheral nervous systems. It often occurs due to the combination of multiple serotonergic agents or an overdose of a single agent.

## Clinical Triad
Diagnosis is based on the presence of three key feature sets:
1. **Mental Status Changes:** Agitation, confusion, restlessness, delirium.
2. **Autonomic Instability:** Hyperthermia, hypertension, tachycardia, diaphoresis, mydriasis (dilated pupils), vomiting/diarrhea.
3. **Neuromuscular Excitability:** Hyperreflexia, ankle clonus, tremor, myoclonus.

## Common Precipitating Agents
- **SSRIs/SNRIs:** Fluoxetine, Sertraline, Venlafaxine.
- **TCAs:** Clomipramine.
- **MAOIs:** Phenelzine, Linezolid (has MAOI properties).
- **Opioids:** Tramadol, Meperidine.
- **Other substances:** Dextromethorphan (antitussive), MDMA, amphetamines.

## Management
1. **Immediate Discontinuation:** Stop all serotonergic medications.
2. **Supportive Care:**
   - **Hydration:** IV fluids.
   - **Cooling:** Evaporative and convective cooling (fanning, water mist). *Note: Antipyretics are not effective because hyperthermia is due to muscle contraction.*
   - **Blood Pressure Control:** Short-acting antihypertensives (e.g., esmolol) for severe hypertension.
3. **Symptom Control:**
   - **Benzodiazepines (e.g., Lorazepam):** First-line for agitation, decreasing muscle contraction (which lowers temperature), and stabilizing vitals.
4. **Specific Antidote:**
   - **Cyproheptadine:** A 5-HT1A and 5-HT2A receptor antagonist; used if patients are unresponsive to benzodiazepines and supportive care.
5. **Critical Care:** Intubation and sedation for severe hyperthermia ($>41.1^\circ\text{C} / 106^\circ\text{F}$).

## Differential: Serotonin Toxicity vs. NMS

| Feature | Serotonin Toxicity | Neuroleptic Malignant Syndrome (NMS) |
|---|---|---|
| **Precipitant** | Serotonergic drugs | Dopamine antagonists (Antipsychotics) |
| **Muscle Tone** | Hyperreflexia, Clonus | "Lead-pipe" Rigidity |
| **Pupils** | Mydriasis (Dilated) | Usually normal |
| **Onset** | Rapid | Slower |
| **Treatment** | Cyproheptadine, BZDs | Bromocriptine, Dantrolene |
