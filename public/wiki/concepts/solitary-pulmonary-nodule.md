# Solitary Pulmonary Nodule (SPN)

## Summary
A solitary pulmonary nodule is a round opacity $\le 3$ cm in diameter, surrounded by pulmonary parenchyma, without associated pleural effusion, adenopathy, or atelectasis.

## Evaluation Algorithm
The primary goal is to differentiate benign from malignant nodules.

1. **Step 1: Comparison with Previous Imaging**
   - Obtain any prior radiographs/CTs.
   - **Stable for $> 2$ years:** Malignancy effectively ruled out $\rightarrow$ no further testing.
   - **No prior imaging available:** Proceed to Step 2.

2. **Step 2: Characterization via CT Scan**
   - Provides detailed information on size, borders, and density.
   - **Features favoring malignancy:**
     - Large size
     - Low density
     - Spiculated borders
     - Eccentric calcification
   - **Patient risk factors:** Age $> 40$, smoking history, weight loss, prior malignancy.

3. **Step 3: Further Diagnostic / Therapeutic Action**
   - **High risk:** Surgical excision.
   - **Low risk:** Serial CT scans (interval depends on characteristics).
   - **Intermediate risk:** FDG-PET scan or tissue biopsy.
     - **Central lesions:** Bronchoscopy.
     - **Peripheral lesions:** CT-guided percutaneous biopsy.
