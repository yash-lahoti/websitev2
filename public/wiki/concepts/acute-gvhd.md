# Acute Graft-versus-Host Disease (GVHD)

## Summary
GVHD is a complication of allogeneic bone marrow transplantation where donor T-lymphocytes recognize and attack the host's HLA antigens.

## Pathophysiology
- **Mechanism:** Recognition of host major and minor HLA-antigens by **donor T-cells** $\rightarrow$ cell-mediated immune response.
- **Contrast:** This is opposite to graft rejection, where host T-cells attack donor cells.

## Clinical Presentation
The "classic triad" of target organs:
1. **Skin:** Maculopapular pruritic rash. Typically starts on the face, palms, and soles, and may generalize.
2. **Gastrointestinal Tract:** Profuse, watery diarrhea; often positive for occult blood.
3. **Liver:** Abnormal LFTs (elevated bilirubin, alkaline phosphatase) and jaundice.

## Incidence
- Up to 50% of patients receiving bone marrow transplants from HLA-matched siblings develop acute GVHD.
