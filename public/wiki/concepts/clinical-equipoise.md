# Clinical Equipoise

## Summary
Clinical equipoise is the state of genuine uncertainty within the expert medical community regarding the comparative effectiveness or safety of two or more acceptable treatments.

## Role in Research Ethics
- **Requirement for RCTs:** Randomization in a Randomized Controlled Trial (RCT) is ethically permissible **only** if clinical equipoise exists.
- **Rationale:** If one treatment is already known to be superior, randomizing a patient to the inferior arm would violate the physician's duty to provide the best possible care and would compromise the participant's welfare.
- **Purpose of the RCT:** The goal of the trial is to resolve this uncertainty and establish which treatment is superior.

## Ethical Control Arm Requirements
- **Standard of Care:** In trials for treatable, progressive diseases, the control arm should reflect the current accepted standard of care.
- **Placebo Use:** An inactive placebo is only acceptable when:
    1. No effective standard of care exists.
    2. Withholding treatment would not result in significant harm to the participants.

## Stopping a Trial
- An RCT should be stopped if interim data analysis reveals a clear, highly significant benefit or harm associated with one of the arms, as equipoise is no longer maintained.
